Fixed a clipped toast shadow and made "friend is playing" say the right thing depending on whose game just started.

## Fixed

- 👻 **The toast shadow was still clipped, just top and bottom this time** — the previous fix widened the left/right margin, but the window had zero room above and below the card, so the shadow's glow got cut off there instead. Fixed properly now.
- 🎮 **"Friend is playing" now says the right thing from either side** — if you're already in and a friend joins, it still reads "X just started Y." But if you're the one joining and they were already there, it now says "X is playing Y" instead of implying they just launched it.
- 🔔 **"Friend is playing" no longer sits in the notification bell** — it only ever mattered in the moment, while you're already looking at the game, so a permanent bell entry was always stale by the time anyone read it. Still a toast, just not a lasting one.
