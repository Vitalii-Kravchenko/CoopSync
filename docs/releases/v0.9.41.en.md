Online presence, a per-game "only me / shared" sync scope, and a round of bug fixes around both.

## New

- 👥 **See when your co-op partner is online** — a dot on their avatar (now in the top-right corner) plus a status label. One GitHub login now covers everything; no separate sign-in step just for presence.
- 🔒 **Per-game sync scope** — the "Only me" / "Shared with a friend" toggle now works for ANY game, not just extra folders. Keep a specific game's saves private to yourself even while your main repo stays shared with a friend.
- Removing a shared game or extra folder now gives your co-op partner a Restore option instead of silently wiping their local copy.
- Removing an extra folder now actually deletes its content from the shared repo too, instead of just dropping the local reference and quietly leaving it taking up space.
- Save location and sync scope now live in one combined, more compact card, with a redesigned "Only me"/"Shared" switch.

## Fixed

- Uninstalling CoopSync no longer wipes every local setting (save-path overrides, per-game sync scope, language, avatar) — only removing a game yourself, or deleting the app's data folder, does that now.
- Switching a shared custom game to "only for me" no longer unshares it for your co-op partner too.
- Toggling sync scope with no actual gameplay change no longer bumps (or drops) the version number; real progress made while "only for me" now properly reaches the shared history once you switch back.
- Fixed a race that could add an extra folder twice.
- Buttons no longer let their label text get selected by a fast or repeated click.
- Fixed a rendering glitch where the "Only me"/"Shared" switch's loading spinner briefly distorted the button shape.
