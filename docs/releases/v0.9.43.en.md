A smarter "friend is playing" notification, and update-download reliability fixes.

## Fixed

- 🎮 **"Friend is playing" notifications are no longer noisy** — they used to fire for any game a friend touched, even ones you don't play yourself. Now they only interrupt when it's a game you're currently playing too. The Friends tab always shows what a friend is up to, regardless — check anytime without being interrupted.
- 🔄 **Download update, fixed** — clicking "Download update" twice (in Settings, the banner, or the toast) could start two overlapping downloads, which is what made the shown progress jump around instead of climbing steadily. The button is now blocked after the first click, and a stalled download recovers on its own after a short wait instead of getting stuck forever.
