A redesigned card for extra save folders, plus a round of custom-game bug fixes.

## New

- 🗂️ **Extra save folders, redesigned** — a folder's card now shows just its name, ownership badge, sync status, and Upload/Download at a glance. The save path, the "Only me"/"Shared" switch, file exclusions, and removing the folder now live behind a settings toggle, since they're set once and rarely touched again. Cards sit two per row on a wide window and collapse to one full-width row when there isn't room for both.

## Fixed

- The step indicator in "Add a custom game" no longer looks like its connecting line and second step have drifted off to the right.
- The modal's close button, and the "add a cover" area, now use the same hover style as the rest of the app (and an actual pointer cursor).
- The excluded-files list now refreshes automatically when you change a game's save path, instead of still showing the previous folder's files.
- An extra folder's "Only me"/"Shared with a friend" buttons are the right way round now, and sized to their text instead of stretched to half the card each.
- The "Custom game" badge next to a game's sync status now lines up with it instead of sitting higher.
- Several rows on a custom game's detail screen (header, save path, danger zone) wrap onto their own line instead of getting squeezed when the window is narrow.
- The "Auto-sync .exe" and "Exclude files" cards on a custom game's detail screen now match height, and actually separate onto their own rows once the window's too narrow for both side by side.
