A live "friend is playing" badge, custom in-app toast notifications, and privacy/terms pages.

## New

- 🎮 **See when a friend starts playing** — a live badge lights up on the game's card the instant a mutual friend launches something you both sync, plus a toast/bell notification. Pushed the moment they launch — no waiting on the background poll.
- 🔔 **Custom toast notifications** — replaced the plain Windows Action Center popup with a styled overlay that matches the app, for every notification kind (friend uploaded a save, update available, new games, friend accepted/declined an invite, sync conflict, access revoked, game/folder removed).
- 📄 **Privacy Policy and Terms** — added, reviewed against GDPR and Polish law, linked from Settings → About in your language.
