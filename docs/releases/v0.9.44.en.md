The update popup now tracks the whole download live, and "friend is playing" won't double up anymore.

## Fixed

- 🔔 **"Friend is playing" could fire twice** — a friend's presence re-announcing itself (a periodic background refresh, or a brief reconnect) could trigger the same notification a second time for the exact same session. It's now deduplicated per friend per game, and resets properly when they — or you — actually leave.
- 🔄 **The update popup now tracks the whole download live** — it used to freeze with a "Download update" button even after the update had already been downloaded (or installed) from Settings or the Games tab. Now it reflects the real state as it happens: progress while downloading, "Install update" once it's ready — and it gets out of the way on its own if you start the download somewhere else instead.
- 🖥️ **No more duplicate update notice on the Games tab** — if the banner there is already telling you about it, the popup at the bottom of the screen won't show up saying the same thing again. It only appears when you're not already looking at the banner.
- 👻 **Fixed a clipped shadow on the popup notifications** — the glow around the card was getting cut off on the left and right instead of fading out smoothly.
