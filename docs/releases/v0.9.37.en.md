Your save now syncs while you're still playing, not just when you quit.

## New

- ⚡ **Saves upload automatically during play, seconds after you save** — CoopSync watches your save folder and pushes as soon as a save has genuinely finished writing (it waits for the file to go quiet first, so a save that's still being written is never uploaded half-done). You no longer have to close the game for your progress to reach your co-op partner — and if you quit and shut down your PC right after, there's a much smaller window where that last save hasn't made it to the cloud yet. The push on quit is still there too, as a final check.
