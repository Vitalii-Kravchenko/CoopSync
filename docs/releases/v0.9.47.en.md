## Fixed

- 🔔 **The update-available toast popped up even while Settings already showed the same thing** — the Games tab banner was already exempted from the toast for that exact reason, but Settings' own "Download update" row in the About card wasn't, so a manual "Check for updates" click there (or the periodic background check) still triggered a redundant toast on top of it.
